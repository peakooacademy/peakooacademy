In Short

We collect only what we need to enrol you, teach you and support you: your name, WhatsApp number, email, course choice and payment status.

We never sell your personal data. Payments go through a payment gateway, so we do not see your card or UPI PIN.

You can ask us to correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by writing to success@peakoo.agency.

This summary is a plain-language guide. The full text below applies.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Peakoo Academy, operated by Peakoo Agency, an individual business, in Goa, India (“we”, “us”), handles your personal data when you visit academy.peakoo.agency, book our masterclass, enrol in a course, join our community or contact us. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), we are the Data Fiduciary for the data described here.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Details you give us: name, WhatsApp number, email address, the course or offer you choose, your masterclass slot, and billing details needed for a payment receipt.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive the transaction ID, amount and payment status. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or net-banking password.
  • Learning and community data: attendance, assignments, project and portfolio links, feedback, and messages you post in our WhatsApp or Discord groups. Live sessions may be recorded, and if you speak or switch on your camera, your voice or image may appear in the recording.
  • Messages to us: your chats, emails and call notes when you contact support or the admissions team.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, referring page and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar tools as described in our Cookies Policy.

3. Why we use your data

  • to register you, confirm your masterclass slot or seat, and send joining details;
  • to deliver courses, give access to lessons, run live classes and give mentor feedback;
  • to process payments, issue invoices and keep accounting and tax records;
  • to answer your questions and send important service messages (for example schedule changes);
  • to moderate the community and keep learners safe;
  • to send updates, offers and news about new batches, only where you have agreed, with an easy way to stop;
  • to understand how the website is used and improve it;
  • to prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.

4. Consent and legal basis

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you submit a form, pay for a programme, join a group or accept optional cookies. We may also process data for the “legitimate uses” the DPDP Act permits, such as responding to a request you made, completing a transaction you started, or complying with the law.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. If you withdraw consent that we need to provide a service, we may not be able to continue that service. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Academy, and only as needed:

  • payment gateway: Razorpay;
  • messaging: WhatsApp (Meta) and email services, to send confirmations and updates;
  • website hosting: Hostinger;
  • live-class and community platforms: Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp and Discord;
  • analytics and advertising partners: only if you accept the relevant cookies: Google Analytics (Google) and Meta Pixel (Meta);
  • professional advisers, auditors, courts or authorities: where the law requires.

These providers act on our instructions or under their own privacy terms.

6. Community and public content

What you post in the community is visible to other members of that group. Please do not share sensitive personal details there. We will feature your name, photo, project or testimonial publicly only after we ask you and you agree.

7. Storing data outside India

Some of our providers (for example Google, Meta, Zoom or Razorpay) may process data on servers outside India. We take reasonable steps to choose providers that protect data, and we transfer data only to the extent permitted by Indian law.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires. In general:

  • enrolment and payment records are kept for the period required by tax, accounting and other laws;
  • enquiries that do not lead to enrolment are kept only as long as we need to respond to you and follow up, and then deleted or anonymised;
  • community and learning records are kept while you are an active learner and for a reasonable period afterwards, unless you ask for earlier deletion and the law allows it.

When data is no longer needed for its purpose and the law does not require us to keep it, we delete or anonymise it. You can also ask us to delete it earlier (see “Your rights”).

9. How we protect your data

We use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access limited to team members who need the data, and trusted service providers. No online system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update your data;
  • ask us to erase your data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • have your grievance addressed (see the last section);
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.

To use these rights, email success@peakoo.agency from the email address you enrolled with, or message us on WhatsApp. We may ask for details to confirm it is you. We aim to respond within 30 days.

11. Children

The Academy is meant for learners aged 18 and above. If a person under 18 wants to join, we need the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian before we process the child’s personal data. We do not track or monitor the behaviour of children, and we do not show them targeted advertising.

12. Marketing messages

If you agree to receive updates or offers, we may send them by WhatsApp or email. You can opt out at any time by replying “STOP”, using the unsubscribe link, or writing to us. We will still send service messages about your enrolment.

13. Links to other websites

Our website and messages may link to other sites (for example WhatsApp, Meta, Google or social media pages). We do not control their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or the law change. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. For material changes we will notify you by email or WhatsApp.
