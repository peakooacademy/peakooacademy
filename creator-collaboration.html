MY JOURNEY BEFORE & AFTER IIT

Like many students, before joining IIT Bombay, I had dreams of getting into a prestigious engineering institution. Preparing for JEE was an important phase of my life. At that time, I thought getting into an IIT was the ultimate achievement.

MY BIGGEST SUGGESTION IS SIMPLE

01

Don’t restrict yourself to academics alone.

Academics build your foundational discipline, but real character, critical thinking, and leadership develop when you step outside the lecture hall.

02

Explore different clubs, sports, cultures, and activities.

Immerse yourself in cultural societies, athletics, tech fests, and student communities to discover what truly excites and challenges you.

03

Try new things, even if you are afraid of failure.

Breakthroughs happen when you dare to tackle unfamiliar challenges. In college, stumbling is just rapid feedback that builds lifelong resilience.

04

Develop communication, leadership, and practical skills.

Learn to articulate ideas persuasively, negotiate, lead high-performing teams, and solve messy real-world problems that textbooks cannot teach.

05

Build something new, whether it is a project, startup, skill, team, or creative idea.

Transition from a passive consumer to a proactive creator. Code software, launch an initiative, build a venture, or create meaningful media.

06

Enjoy your college life and make unforgettable memories.

These transformative years never return. Form deep lifelong friendships, celebrate victories, explore, and craft memories you will cherish forever.
