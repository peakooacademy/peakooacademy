In Short

Your ₹99 masterclass fee is credited in full towards a course if you enrol within 48 hours of the session.

Live courses: full refund if you ask before the first live class starts. Recorded courses: refund only if you ask within 48 hours and have not accessed any lesson.

If we cancel or change a batch, you can choose a full refund, a credit or a transfer.

This summary is a plain-language guide. The full text below applies.

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to everything you buy from Peakoo Academy: the masterclass, individual courses and the bundle. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions. All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and are the total amount you paid. No GST is charged.

2. Masterclass (₹99)

The ₹99 fee is credited in full towards any course or the bundle if you enrol within 48 hours after the masterclass session you booked.

After 48 hours the credit expires. The ₹99 is not refunded in cash.

If we cancel or reschedule your session and you cannot attend the new slot, we will refund the ₹99 in full or move you to another slot, as you prefer.

3. Live courses (Website Development & Deployment, Meta Ads)

Before the first live class starts: you can cancel and receive a full refund (less any non-recoverable payment gateway charges).

After the first class has started: the fee is non-refundable because seats are limited and mentors reserve time for your batch. If you tell us before your second class, you may move once, free of charge, to a later batch, subject to seat availability.

If you miss a class, we will share a recording where one is available. Missed classes are not a reason for a refund.

4. Recorded courses (Graphic Designing, Google Business Profile Setup with AI)

These courses give instant digital access. You can ask for a full refund within 48 hours of purchase, but only if you have not opened or played any lesson.

Once you have accessed the content, the fee is non-refundable.

If you cannot access the course because of a fault on our side that we do not fix within 7 days of your report, you are entitled to a full refund.

5. Bundle (all four courses)

The bundle is treated course by course. We work out each course’s share of what you paid in proportion to its listed price, and apply the live and recorded rules above to each share. A recorded course you have accessed or a live batch that has started is not refundable.

6. If we cancel or change something

If we cancel a batch, or make a major change to a live course (for example the format, the number of classes or the start date by more than 14 days) and you cannot continue, you can choose a full refund, a credit for a later batch, or a transfer to another course of the same or lower price.

7. Duplicate, failed or excess payments

If you were charged twice or paid more than the listed price, we will refund the extra amount within 7 working days of confirming it. If a payment failed but your account was debited, the bank or gateway normally reverses it automatically within 5 to 7 working days. Please email us the transaction ID if it does not.

8. When a refund is not available

  • You changed your mind after the windows described above.
  • You did not attend or complete the course, or could not give the time needed.
  • You were not able to buy or use a paid tool. Free alternatives are suggested in class where possible.
  • Your results or income are different from what you expected. Please see the Disclaimer.
  • We ended your access because you broke the rules in the Terms, for example by sharing logins or content, or by harassing others.

9. How to request a refund

Email success@peakoo.agency or message us on WhatsApp with:

  • your full name and the WhatsApp number or email you enrolled with;
  • the course and the payment receipt or transaction ID;
  • the reason for the request.

We will acknowledge your request within 2 working days and tell you the decision within 7 working days.

10. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers usually take 7 to 10 working days after we approve the refund, and we cannot speed that up. We will update your payment receipt where applicable.

11. Please contact us first

If you have a problem with a payment, please write to us before raising a dispute with your bank so that we can resolve it quickly.

12. Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version in force when you paid applies to your purchase.
